Who is Watanyoo Putta?

Watanyoo Putta (วทัญญู พุทธา) is a Thai medical researcher and high school student at Khonkaenwittayayon School in Khon Kaen, Thailand. He works on tropical disease, One Health and biosensors, completed a research internship at the Tropical Disease Research Center, Khon Kaen University, and writes about public health on Medium (@watanyoo.putta).

In 2026 he won first place at the MedSpark Innovation Competition for rapid point-of-care diagnostic biosensors aimed at snake venom detection.
