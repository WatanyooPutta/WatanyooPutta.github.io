Operational Guidelines for Student First Aid Volunteers: Khon Kaen Wittayayon School Health Services (B.E. 2569)

คู่มือการปฏิบัติงานสำหรับอาสาพยาบาล งานพยาบาลโรงเรียนขอนแก่นวิทยายน พ.ศ. 2569

The "Operational Guidelines for Student First Aid Volunteers" serves as the official operational manual and standard protocol for student first aid responders within Khon Kaen Wittayayon School. Designed to support school health services, emergency triage, and peer-led medical care, this manual outlines standard operating procedures (SOPs) for school campus first response. Key topics covered include initial patient assessment, minor wound care, acute illness management, emergency medical referral protocols, equipment inventory management, and volunteer operational ethics.

Authored by Watanyoo Putta, this guide standardises student volunteer workflows to ensure prompt, safe, and effective first-aid assistance across the school campus during academic sessions and extracurricular events.

Published for Khon Kaen Wittayayon School Health Services (B.E. 2569 / 2026).
