Research & Innovation

The Effects of Industrial Noise on Human Mental Health and Well-being

Independent Study (IS) investigating construction-generated noise impacts in school environments. Published openly on Zenodo with DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23219819.

Machine Learning & Snake Venom Biosensors

Awarded Winner of the Minimal Invasive Medical Device Competition by the National Research Council of Thailand (NRCT). Developed an electrochemical biosensor integrated with machine learning to rapidly generate venom protein fingerprints, aiding point-of-care antivenom selection in resource-limited clinical settings.

Tropical Disease Research

Completed a research internship at the Tropical Disease Research Centre, Khon Kaen University (Faculty of Medicine). Research focused on Opisthorchis viverrini (Southeast Asian liver fluke) utilising cell culture, gel electrophoresis, and Western blot techniques.

Satellite Telemetry (TinyGS)

Licensed Basic Amateur Radio Operator (Callsign: E25ZVB). Operates the active 433 MHz TinyGS ground station SatFinder_KKN_KKW to receive and distribute live CubeSat telemetry to a global open-source network.

Public Health & Clinical Work

Active contributor to prehospital care, emergency medical response, and community welfare. Serves as a volunteer coordinating blood and organ donation initiatives for the Thai Red Cross Society and acts as a Social Worker under the Ministry of Social Development and Human Security (MSDHS). Completed clinical shadowing at Nong Hin Hospital and volunteer medical support for the Khon Kaen International Marathon.
