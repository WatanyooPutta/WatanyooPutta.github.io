Professional Overview

Watanyoo Putta (วทัญญู พุทธา) is a Thai medical researcher and student at Khonkaenwittayayon School in Khon Kaen, Thailand. He works on tropical disease, One Health and diagnostic biosensors, completed a research internship at the Tropical Disease Research Center, Khon Kaen University.
