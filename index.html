Professional Overview

Watanyoo Putta is a high school student at Khonkaenwittayayon School and an aspiring physician-scientist based in Khon Kaen, Thailand. Specialising in tropical medicine, biosensors, disease surveillance, and the One Health framework, he completed a formal research internship at the Tropical Disease Research Center, Khon Kaen University, and publishes public science insights on Medium (@watanyoo.putta).
