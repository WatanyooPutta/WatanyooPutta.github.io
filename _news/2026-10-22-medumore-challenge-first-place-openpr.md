Secondary school students representing Khon Kaen Wittayayon School placed first in all four online qualifying rounds of the "MedUMORE" academic competition (Thai: หมอขอชาเลนจ์), organised by the Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University, in collaboration with Workpoint Entertainment.

The two-student team, Watanyoo Putta (M.6/22) and Panisara Wongsanga (M.6/3), was supervised by advisor Ms Supisara Chanthrakhot. The online rounds assessed secondary school teams from across Thailand on healthcare fundamentals, medical technology applications and analytical reasoning under time constraints.

The team has advanced to the on-site national final at the Workpoint Studio in Pathum Thani Province on 12 to 13 December 2026. The televised broadcast will air on Workpoint TV (Channel 23) on 13, 20 and 27 January 2027.
