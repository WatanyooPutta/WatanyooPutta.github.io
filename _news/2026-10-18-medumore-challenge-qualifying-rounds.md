Students from Khon Kaen Wittayayon School ranked first in all four online qualifying rounds of the "MedUMORE" academic competition (Thai: หมอขอชาเลนจ์), organised by the Faculty of Medicine, Chulalongkorn University.

The team advanced to the on-site final at the Workpoint studio on 12–13 December 2026. The programme is scheduled to air on 13, 20 and 27 January 2027.

- Mr. Watanyoo Putta, Grade 12 (M.6/22)
