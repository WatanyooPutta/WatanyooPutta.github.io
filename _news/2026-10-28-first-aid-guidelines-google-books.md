"Operational Guidelines for Student First Aid Volunteers: Khon Kaen Wittayayon School Health Services (B.E. 2569)" (Thai: คู่มือการปฏิบัติงานสำหรับอาสาพยาบาล งานพยาบาลโรงเรียนขอนแก่นวิทยายน พ.ศ. 2569) is a 164-page manual by Watanyoo Putta, now available on Google Books.

It sets out standard operating procedures for student first aid responders at Khon Kaen Wittayayon School: initial patient assessment, minor wound care, acute illness management, emergency medical referral, equipment inventory management and volunteer ethics.

Published for Khon Kaen Wittayayon School Health Services (B.E. 2569 / 2026).
